# 论文文献对比段落

## 方法定位（中文）

CuPL直接使用大语言模型生成的类别描述进行训练自由的零样本分类。本文方法则先用16-shot Base类图像学习CoOp提示，再在推理阶段将学习提示后验与冻结类别描述后验进行固定权重的概率融合。因此，本文不是训练自由方法；它研究的是任务特定提示与通用语言先验之间的互补。融合模块本身不增加可训练参数，整个方法仍包含CoOp的2,048个可训练提示参数。

## Related work（英文，可直接修改后使用）

CuPL enriches zero-shot CLIP with class-specific descriptions generated by a large language model, without optimizing the vision-language model on labeled images. In contrast, our method learns a task-specific prompt from 16-shot base-class images and combines its posterior with a frozen description-based posterior using a fixed probability mixture. The two branches are complementary: the learned prompt improves adaptation to the downstream task, whereas the frozen descriptions retain semantic knowledge that transfers to novel classes. The fusion module introduces no trainable parameters beyond the 2,048 context parameters of CoOp.

## 结果分析（英文，可直接修改后使用）

Across UCF101, DTD, FGVC-Aircraft, EuroSAT, and Flowers102, our method obtains an average harmonic mean of 70.13. This result is higher than the published five-dataset averages of CuPL (61.76) and KgCoOp (67.19), and is comparable to MaPLe (70.07), while remaining below PromptSRC (72.58). Unlike methods that optimize additional deep or multimodal prompts, our fixed probability fusion adds no trainable parameters to the CoOp learner. On an RTX 4090, it increases batch-64 inference latency by only 0.74% and stores a 0.0977 MiB frozen class-feature buffer. These results indicate that the proposed fusion provides a favorable accuracy-efficiency trade-off rather than a new state of the art.

## 表注（英文）

$\dagger$ denotes results reported by the corresponding papers. These methods were not rerun in our codebase. All entries use CLIP ViT-B/16 and the standard base-to-novel class splits, but their optimization schedules and implementation details are not fully identical. CuPL follows the base-to-novel construction in ProText, which uses CuPL descriptions for base classes and zero-shot CLIP for novel classes.

## 禁止使用的表述

- 不写 “the whole method is parameter-free”；应写 “the fusion module adds no trainable parameters”。
- 不写 “outperforms PromptSRC” 或 “state of the art”。
- 不把原论文数字称为本地复现或完全等计算量比较。
- 不把 +0.06pp 描述成显著超过 MaPLe；应写 “comparable to MaPLe”。
